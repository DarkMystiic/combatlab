  1. Pick a style and a level

    Boxing or Muay Thai, from Beginner to Pro. Higher levels call longer combos with less time between them.

  2. Train to the voice

    Four coach voices call each combination the way a trainer holding pads would. Keep your eyes on the bag, not the phone.

  3. Keep the streak

    Every round counts toward your ranks, badges and weekly goal, with a history of when and how hard you trained.

Write it like your coach does.

Type a combo the way it goes on the gym whiteboard: numbers, words or both. CombatLab reads it, checks it and calls it in your next round.
